From the course Canvas page: "Lectures, labs, and assignments
We planned six weeks of meetings, with two meetings per week: lectures and lab meetings. 

The lectures should be prepared by reading the compulsory literature, to be found in the Modules for each week and listed below. Reading the literature as a preparation for the lectures is absolutely necessary to keep up. With each lecture, a few self-test questions are available through Canvas as a quiz. These questions are similar to the ones you can expect at the exam and give an indication of your grasp of the theory. Your answers to the quiz questions are not graded, they are just for you to test your knowledge. There will NOT (!!!!) be a separate practice exam in addition to the quizzes, so use these quizzes to get an idea about the exam questions.

During the lab meetings, you work on the weekly assignment and you practice your text mining skills, which is needed for your final assignment. The weekly assignments and the final assignment are made as a group."
