- **Notation:** the course follows Lewis & Papadimitriou conventions ($e$ for the empty word, not $\varepsilon$; $\triangleright$ tape marker; TM building blocks), not Sipser.
- No instructor name, grading scheme, or exam dates appear in the available slides.

## Course structure

- First ~2/3: **computability theory** - models of computation (finite automata, pushdown automata, Turing machines), undecidability, the halting problem.
- Last ~1/3: **complexity theory** - P, NP, NP-completeness.
- Described as "a rigorous mathematical course with emphasis on theorems and proofs".
