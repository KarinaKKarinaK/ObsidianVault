## Exam rules

- Closed book. One A4 cheat sheet, single or double sided, printed or handwritten. No calculator.
- Algorithms in pseudocode or plain language; define input and output of every subroutine; algorithms from class, homework, or tutorials can be cited as black boxes; modifications must be explained in full.
- No correctness proofs required on this midterm; running time must be justified briefly.
- No makeup midterm (weight shifts to the final only with prior approval). Missing the final is an automatic fail.
- Appeals: homework within one week to the TA who marked the question; midterm and final at the paper-checking session.

## Homework rules

- Single PDF on Canvas, 300 dpi scans minimum. Late: 1 point per hour.
- Discussion and AI allowed, but solutions in your own words (watermark checks), you must be able to explain them, and collaborators or AI must be acknowledged in writing.
- Answer structure the course wants: main idea, pseudocode or description, running-time analysis, correctness proof unless obvious.
