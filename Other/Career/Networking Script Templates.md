# Networking Script Templates

Copy, personalize the [brackets], and send. Source: @hinumpy.

## 1. Cold LinkedIn Connection Request

Use when: reaching out to someone at a company you want to work at, no prior contact.

> Hi [Name], I'm a [year/major] at [school] with a strong interest in [team/role type] at [Company]. I'd love to connect and learn more about your experience there.

Tip: keep it under 300 characters. That's LinkedIn's connection note limit.

## 2. The Follow-Up (After They Accept)

Use when: they accepted your connection request.

> Thank you for connecting, [Name]. I saw that you work on [specific team/project], which is exactly the kind of work I'm hoping to break into. Would you be open to a quick 15-minute chat sometime in the next couple of weeks? Happy to work around your schedule.

Tip: mention something specific from their profile. Generic asks get ignored.

## 3. Asking for a Referral

Use when: you've built some rapport (post-coffee chat or a warmer connection) and a role is open.

> Hi [Name], I hope you've been well. I noticed [Company] has an opening for [Role] ([link]), and I believe my background in [1 to 2 relevant skills/projects] could be a strong fit. Would you be comfortable referring me, or pointing me to the right person to speak with? Happy to send my resume over, no pressure either way.

Tip: always attach or link your resume in the same message so they don't have to ask for it.

## 4. Coffee Chat Request

Use when: cold or warm outreach, asking for someone's time to learn about their path.

> Hi [Name], I came across your profile and admired your path from [their background] to [current role/company]. I'm currently [your situation] and would love to hear how you got there, if you have 15 minutes for a call sometime soon.

Tip: offer 2 to 3 specific time windows instead of "whenever works". It reduces friction to book.

## 5. Thank-You Note (Post Coffee Chat / Interview)

Use when: right after any call, chat, or interview.

> Thank you so much for taking the time to chat today, [Name]. I really enjoyed hearing about [something specific they said], and it gave me a lot to think about for [your next step]. I appreciate you sharing your time and advice, and I hope to stay in touch.

Tip: send this within 24 hours while the conversation is still fresh for them.

## 6. Re-Engaging a Dormant Connection

Use when: someone you spoke with months ago, checking back in.

> Hi [Name], it's been a while since we last talked. I hope things have been going well at [Company]. I wanted to reach back out because [reason: new opening / update on your end / etc.]. Would love to reconnect whenever you have a few minutes.

Tip: always give them a reason for reaching out again, not just "checking in".
