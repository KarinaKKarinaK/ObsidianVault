## Company Profiles: The Most Important Ones to Know

### Boston Dynamics
- Founded by Marc Raibert as MIT spinoff (1992)
- Originally DARPA-funded military research
- Acquired by Google (2013) → SoftBank (2017) → Hyundai (2021)
- Products: Spot (quadruped, ~$75k), Atlas (humanoid, commercial 2024), Stretch (warehouse)
- Known for viral videos of robots doing parkour, dancing, gymnastics
- Now serious commercial business — Spot deployed at construction sites, nuclear plants, oil & gas

### Physical Intelligence (π)
- Founded 2024 by Sergey Levine, Chelsea Finn, Karol Hausman, Brian Ichter (all from Google Brain/DeepMind)
- Raised $70M seed + $400M Series A + further rounds = $1.1B+
- Philosophy: build the "brain" (VLA software), let hardware partners build the body
- Partners include Apptronik, Boston Dynamics, others
- π0 trained on diverse robot types; π0.5 adds language instruction following improvements

### Figure AI
- Founded 2022 by Brett Adcock (serial entrepreneur)
- Raised $675M in 2024 (Microsoft, Nvidia, Jeff Bezos, OpenAI, Intel, Samsung)
- Then ended partnership with OpenAI; developed own AI in-house
- Figure 02 deployed at BMW Spartanburg plant for automotive assembly
- BotQ: first purpose-built robot factory (Sunnyvale), targeting 12,000 units/year
- Valuation reached $39B by September 2025

### Unitree Robotics
- Chinese company offering by far cheapest capable robots
- Go2 quadruped (~$3,000) massively adopted in research
- G1 humanoid at ~$16,000 — 5x cheaper than competitors
- H1 humanoid used widely in academic research
- Risk: IP and national security concerns for US organizations
